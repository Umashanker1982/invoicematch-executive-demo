# InvoiceMatch: Executive Stakeholder Demo & Productization Workshop

**Lab: Executive Stakeholder Demo & Productization Workshop** · by **Umashanker**

An executive-ready demo of **InvoiceMatch**, an AI invoice-to-PO matching product, followed by a productization workshop that turns stakeholder feedback into a launch decision and a plan.

## Recommendation: 🟡 LAUNCH WITH CONDITIONS
A 12-week pilot that starts in shadow mode. Auto-approval is switched on only after the security review, a shadow-mode accuracy check on real invoices, and the operational drills are passed. → [executive_summary.md](executive_summary.md)

## How to review (about 15 minutes)

| Step | Open | What you'll see | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **[executive_summary.md](executive_summary.md)** | One page: problem, evidence, recommendation, conditions, decisions needed | 2 min |
| 2 | **[demo_deck.pdf](demo_deck.pdf)** | The 8 presentation slides (with backup screenshots of the live demo) | 3 min |
| 3 | **[demo_script.md](demo_script.md)** | The minute-by-minute 7-minute demo, including complete / incomplete / risky / manual | 3 min |
| 4 | **Live product:** https://invoicematch.onrender.com | Follow the script yourself: select "Claude via SharedLLM", then click Quick tour 1, 2, and 5 (the first visit may take about 50 seconds to wake up) | 3 min |
| 5 | **[feedback_log.md](feedback_log.md)** | 10 feedback items, questions, and objections with owners and dates; 4 decisions; 3 launch conditions | 2 min |
| 6 | **[productization_plan.md](productization_plan.md)** | Launch scope, release criteria, users, onboarding, HEART metrics, support, operations, risks, dependencies, prioritized backlog | 3 min |

**Demo recording or presentation artifact:** this submission uses the **presentation artifact** option: the slide deck ([demo_deck.pdf](demo_deck.pdf)) plus the **working product** that reviewers can run themselves with the script. No recording is included.
